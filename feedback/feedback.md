# User Feedback — Bite Balance

- **How collected:** Chat conversation (1 user)
- **When:** 2026-07-24

## Raw feedback

1. The tester loved the dark theme and said the app felt premium. She especially liked the BMI card on the home screen — the colors made it immediately clear where she stood.

2. She praised the login and register pages, noting that the large brand panel on the left looked great. The smooth animations were a nice touch too.

3. On the Dashboard, she was confused by a large plus button she couldn't see at first — it was the same color as the background. She had to tap around to find it.

4. When she typed a wrong email format during sign-up, the error just said "Something went wrong" — she had no idea what to fix.

5. She liked how the app knew her BMI right away and gave her a personalized calorie target. It felt tailored to her.

6. On the Analytics page, some error text spilled outside its box on her small phone screen.

7. Her overall take: the app looks and feels really polished. Just a few tiny rough edges to smooth out.

## Themes (what keeps coming up)

- **Visual polish is a standout** — dark theme, animations, premium feel
- **Personalization works** — BMI card and calorie targets feel tailored
- **A few UI tweaks needed** — some buttons hide in the background, some text overflows

## Top 3 things to fix

- [ ] Change the Dashboard plus button color so it stands out from the background
- [ ] Tweak the error message text on sign-up to say something like "That doesn't look like a valid email"
- [ ] Shrink the error text font on small screens so it fits inside its container
